Privacy notice and consent

Draft for the DSCE ISE community project. To be reviewed and approved by the project guide before any real pilot.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, and a securely hashed password (never stored in plain text).
  • Complaint: photo(s), short description, observation time, the location you set, optional landmark, and category.
  • Activity: status history, notes, feedback you send, and security logs (without passwords or session tokens).

We do not ask for Aadhaar, government ID, phone number or home address. Please do not include them in descriptions.

How photos and locations are handled

  • Photos are re-encoded on upload. This removes embedded metadata (EXIF), including any GPS position stored by the camera. We do not read GPS from photos.
  • Your browser's location is used only if you press "Use my location" and grant permission.
  • Original photos and exact coordinates are visible only to you and project reviewers/administrators.
  • Public pages show only aggregate counts. Original photos are never published; there is no automatic face or number-plate blurring, so unreviewed images are not released.
  • The system does not perform face recognition or number-plate recognition.

Retention (pilot policy)

Pilot data is kept only for the academic project period (2026–27) and deleted or anonymised afterwards, unless the team's guide approves a different policy. You may ask the project team to delete your account and complaints.

Consent statement

By submitting a complaint I confirm that:

  1. I took the photo(s) or have the right to share them, and I tried to avoid identifiable people and number plates.
  2. I agree that the photo, description and location will be stored and reviewed by the CivicEye project team for this academic pilot.
  3. I understand that CivicEye is an academic prototype, not an official municipal portal or emergency service, and that no repair is guaranteed.
