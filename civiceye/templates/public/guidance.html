Reporting guidance and limitations

How to report safely

  • Do not stand in traffic to take a photo. Your safety comes first.
  • Frame the issue itself. Avoid faces, number plates, house numbers and people's homes where possible.
  • Use your device location only if you are at the spot. Otherwise click the map to set the location, and correct it if the pin is wrong.
  • Describe what you saw in a sentence or two. Do not include names, phone numbers or ID numbers.
  • Check the "possible existing reports nearby" panel. If the same issue is already reported you may still submit; a reviewer decides whether reports are duplicates.

Limitations

  • No trained image model is installed in this build. The app shows "AI unavailable" instead of guessing.
  • Category suggestions from the description use a keyword rule, not a trained model. The score is not a probability.
  • The app never estimates pothole depth, size, repair cost or danger from a photo. Risk is assessed by a human reviewer.
  • Priority scores are decision support. The weights are a design choice and have not been validated.
  • "Resolved" means a project reviewer marked it resolved. Unless resolution evidence is attached, it is not independently verified.
  • Not an emergency service. For emergencies call 112.
