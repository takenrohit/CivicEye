CivicEye

An AI-assisted traffic, pothole and civic complaint analysis prototype for a proposed pilot locality around DSCE, Kumaraswamy Layout, Bengaluru.

Residents can photograph and locate civic issues (potholes, garbage, waterlogging, streetlights and others). Project reviewers check each report, confirm its category and risk, look for nearby duplicates and record every status change with a reason.

Please read before using

  • This is an academic decision-support prototype, not an official government complaint portal.
  • It is not an emergency service and does not guarantee any repair.
  • Reviewer and "officer" accounts are simulated project roles unless authorised participation is arranged.
  • Automatic image recognition is not active in this build. Category suggestions come from a simple keyword rule.
